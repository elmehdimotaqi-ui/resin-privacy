# Epoxy Resin Calculator — Support

Epoxy Resin Calculator works out how much resin to mix and how to split it into part A and part B: mould volume
(rectangle, cylinder, dome, sphere, cone, pyramid, ring, a volume you know, or water fill), Mix only, tabletop and
tumbler coats, river tables with a layer plan, a batch of several moulds in one mix, pigment, cost, a unit converter,
your own material profiles, projects with a pour sheet, and a pot-life timer.

## Common questions

### Why is my total a little more than I expected?

Two things add to what the mould holds:

- **Waste.** Mould, coat, river and batch results add **10 %** for what stays in the cup and on the stir stick. Change
  it on the screen or in **Settings → Waste margin**. Mix only adds nothing: you type what you need.
- **Rounding up.** Every amount to mix is rounded **up**, never down, and part B is worked out from the rounded part A
  so the two always keep your ratio. You may mix a gram more; you will never come up short.

### Why does it show grams for one product and millilitres for another?

**Weight is truth.** Grams per part are shown only when your product gives a ratio **by weight** (for example
100 : 83 by weight). For a product mixed **by volume** (for example 1 : 1), the app shows millilitres or fluid ounces
per part, because resin and hardener rarely weigh the same per millilitre: equal grams of a 1 : 1-by-volume epoxy can
be about 20 % off ratio. If your datasheet gives both ratios, add the weight ratio to the material and the app shows
both, plus the scale readout.

### What is "no tare" on the scale readout?

Put the empty cup on the scale and pour part A **until** the first number, then add part B **until** the second
number. You don't need to reset the scale between parts. The amounts of each part are shown too, if you prefer to tare.

### Which density does it use?

Total volume and total weight are converted with the material's **mixed density** (1.10 g/mL unless the material says
otherwise). It is always shown next to a converted number, and you can change it on the screen or in the material.

### What does "Small batch: use a scale that reads 0.1 g" mean?

For small pours, rounding to whole grams would move the ratio more than 1 %, so the app switches to tenths of a gram
(or hundredths of an ounce). Use a scale that shows that precision, or mix a bigger batch.

### US units or metric?

The chip at the top of the home screen switches between **US · in** (inches, fl oz, oz) and **metric** (cm and mm, mL,
g). In **Settings → Units** you can choose lengths and volumes/weights separately, for example inches with grams and
millilitres. What you typed is converted when you switch.

### Are the built-in materials my brand?

The built-in materials (tabletop epoxy, deep pour, casting epoxy by weight, polyurethane, UV resin, acrylic casting
mix, pottery plaster) use values from published manufacturer datasheets, named on each material. **Always check the
ratio on your own bottle.** Edit any value, or add your own product in **Pigment · Cost · Converter · Materials →
Materials**.

### How do projects work?

On any calculator, tap **Add to project**. The project's **pour sheet** adds everything up per material and splits it
once, with the scale readout, pigment and cost. Tap an item to reopen its calculator with its values. The first project
is free; more projects unlock with one short video.

### What does watching a video unlock?

Each unlock is **for good, on this device**: more than one project, Batch (several moulds in one mix), the PDF pour
sheet, more than two of your own materials, the pot-life alert notification, and two extra colour themes. One video in
**Settings** also removes ads for 24 hours. There are no purchases and no subscriptions.

### The pot-life notification doesn't appear

The countdown always runs in the app. The notification and the "5 minutes left" alert need the pot-life alert unlocked
and notifications allowed for the app (Android asks the first time you turn it on; you can change it later in your
phone's **Settings → Apps → Epoxy Resin Calculator → Notifications**).

### I reinstalled the app and my projects are gone

Projects and materials are kept **on your device only**: there is no account and no server. If Android backup is on,
they may come back when you restore a backup to a new phone; otherwise uninstalling deletes them. Sharing a pour sheet
as text or PDF is a good way to keep a copy.

### Which languages does it speak?

English, German, French, Spanish and Arabic, following your device language, or the one chosen in **Settings →
Language**. Arabic mirrors the whole layout right-to-left.
