# Epoxy Resin Calculator for Android: Privacy Policy

*Google Play listing: "Epoxy Resin Calculator" (package `com.elmehdi.resincalculator`).
Effective date: 21 October 2026.*

This page explains what the Android app Epoxy Resin Calculator does with information. In short: **your projects,
materials and calculations stay on your phone or tablet. Epoxy Resin Calculator has no account, no sign-in and no
server. Ads come from Google AdMob, and you choose how they may use your data where the law gives you that choice.**

### 1. What stays on your device

Epoxy Resin Calculator stores the following **only on your device**, in the app's private storage:

- your projects and pour sheets: the calculations you added (moulds, coats, river tables, mixes, batches), with the
  amounts, materials, pigment and cost you entered;
- your own material profiles (names, mix ratios, densities, prices) and any changes you made to the built-in ones;
- the last inputs of each calculator, so they are there when you come back;
- the pot-life timer while it runs (when you mixed, which material);
- your settings: units, waste margin, default material, sound, haptics, appearance (light, dark or system), colours
  and language;
- which optional extras you have unlocked, and until when the 24-hour ad-free period runs;
- simple counters (how many times you added to a project, when the rating prompt was last shown), used only to decide
  when to show an ad or Google Play's rating prompt.

The developer never receives any of this. Epoxy Resin Calculator has no analytics of its own. Uninstalling the app (or
Settings → Apps → Epoxy Resin Calculator → Storage → Clear storage) removes everything.

### 2. Android backup

If backup is on for your device, Android may include Epoxy Resin Calculator's projects, materials and settings in
**your device backup in your Google Account**, and may copy them when you move to a new phone. Google handles this
backup under your account; the developer can't see or access it. You can turn backup off in your device's settings.

### 3. Ads (Google AdMob)

Epoxy Resin Calculator is supported by ads from **Google AdMob** (Google Mobile Ads SDK): a banner, an occasional
full-screen ad after you add something to a project, and **rewarded videos you choose to watch**. To serve ads, measure
them and prevent fraud, **Google** may collect and process:

- device identifiers, including the **Android advertising ID**;
- your IP address and the **approximate location** derived from it (Epoxy Resin Calculator never asks for your
  location);
- information about your device and the app (model, OS version, language, app version);
- ad interactions, such as which ads were shown or tapped and whether a rewarded video was completed;
- diagnostic data from the ads SDK, such as performance information.

Google collects this data, not the developer. The developer only sees aggregated reports (for example, how many ads
were shown) and never receives information about you. Google's use of this information is described in the
[Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) and in
[How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).
Ad traffic is encrypted in transit.

### 4. Your choices about ads

- **Consent (EEA, UK, Switzerland and other places where it is required).** Before any ad is requested, Epoxy Resin
  Calculator shows a consent message from Google's User Messaging Platform. You can accept, refuse, or choose purposes
  and partners. If you refuse, Google may still show non-personalised or limited ads, which use less data. The app
  works the same either way.
- **US state privacy laws.** Where they apply, the same message lets you opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of your
  personal information for targeted advertising.
- **Change your choice at any time:** open Epoxy Resin Calculator → **Settings → Privacy options**. The row appears
  whenever your region requires a consent choice and shows the consent message again.
- **Advertising ID:** on your device, go to **Settings → Privacy → Ads** (on some devices **Settings → Google →
  Ads**). You can **reset** or **delete** your advertising ID there.
- **Fewer ads:** watching one rewarded video in Epoxy Resin Calculator's Settings turns off banners and full-screen
  ads for 24 hours.

### 5. Rewarded videos and unlocks

Some optional extras (more projects, Batch, the PDF pour sheet, more own materials, the pot-life alert, extra colour
themes) unlock **for good** when you watch a short rewarded video. These rewards are recorded **only on your device**
(and in your Android backup, if enabled). **Epoxy Resin Calculator sells nothing:** there are no in-app purchases or
subscriptions, and the app never asks for payment details.

### 6. Permissions

Epoxy Resin Calculator asks for **one** runtime permission, and only if you turn on the pot-life alert:

- **Notifications** (`POST_NOTIFICATIONS`, Android 13 and later), to show the pot-life countdown and the "5 minutes
  left" alert while the screen is off. If you say no, the timer still works inside the app.

Its manifest also declares:

- **Internet** (`INTERNET`) and **network state** (`ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE`), to load ads and the consent message and
  to open this policy;
- **Vibrate** (`VIBRATE`), for haptic feedback;
- **Advertising ID** (`AD_ID`), added by the Google Mobile Ads SDK, together with Android's ad services permissions
  (`ACCESS_ADSERVICES_AD_ID`, `ACCESS_ADSERVICES_ATTRIBUTION`, `ACCESS_ADSERVICES_TOPICS`) so it can use Android's
  advertising and Privacy Sandbox APIs. Libraries the ads SDK relies on add **wake lock** and **foreground service**
  to finish short background tasks; Epoxy Resin Calculator itself runs no background service.

Epoxy Resin Calculator doesn't access your location, contacts, camera, microphone, files or photos.

### 7. Sharing

"Share" opens Android's share sheet with a short text of a result or a pour sheet, or with a PDF of the pour sheet
that the app creates in its own temporary storage. You choose the app it goes to, and that app's own policy then
applies. Epoxy Resin Calculator doesn't send it anywhere itself.

### 8. Rating prompt

After you have used the app a few times, Epoxy Resin Calculator may ask Google Play to show its in-app rating
prompt, at most once a month. Google Play runs that prompt; Epoxy Resin Calculator doesn't learn whether you rated it
or what you wrote.

### 9. Children

Epoxy Resin Calculator is not directed at children under 13, and the developer does not knowingly collect personal
information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, contact us at
the address below.

### 10. Keeping and deleting data

The developer holds no data about you, so there is nothing to delete on the developer's side. To remove what is on
your device, delete it in the app or uninstall the app. For data Google processes for ads, use the choices in
section 4 and Google's own controls at [myadcenter.google.com](https://myadcenter.google.com).
